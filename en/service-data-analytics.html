    Data Analytics & Data Science

    We turn scattered data into a clear picture: from collecting and cleaning it to analysing it and presenting it in dashboards that support decisions.

    Request this service WhatsApp (opens in a new tab)

    Value for your business

    A decision based on organized data carries less risk than one based on impressions. Analysis shows you what actually works and where opportunities are lost, and gives you indicators you can track continuously.

    • Clear dashboards
    • Understanding customer behavior
    • Automatable reporting

    What we offer

    • Data collection & cleaning

      Consolidating data sources and removing errors and duplicates.

    • Exploratory data analysis

      Finding patterns and relationships before drawing conclusions.

    • Data visualization

      Charts and views that make numbers easy to understand.

    • Business intelligence dashboards

      Interactive dashboards that bring your key metrics together.

    • KPI monitoring

      Defining the metrics that matter and tracking them regularly.

    • Sales & marketing analytics

      Linking campaigns and sales to see what delivers a return.

    • Customer behavior analysis

      Understanding how customers interact with your products and site.

    • Customer segmentation

      Grouping customers into segments to tailor messages and offers.

    • Marketing attribution analysis

      Estimating each channel’s contribution to results.

    • Forecasting & predictive modeling

      Estimation models, chosen according to your data and project requirements.

    • Statistical analysis

      Tests and measurements that support conclusions.

    • Data-driven decision support

      Turning analysis results into practical recommendations.

    • Reporting automation

      Reports that update automatically instead of repetitive manual work.

    • Database analysis & integration

      Working with databases and data sources where supported.

    Business use cases

    • Improving campaign performance by knowing what actually delivers results
    • Understanding customer behavior and the path to purchase
    • Identifying sales trends and seasonality
    • Reducing waste and operational inefficiencies

    Tools we work with

    • Python
    • SQL
    • Power BI
    • Excel
    • Google Analytics 4

    Scope of work

    The tools and techniques used (including predictive models) are chosen according to your data and the project’s goals, and confirmed in the service proposal.
