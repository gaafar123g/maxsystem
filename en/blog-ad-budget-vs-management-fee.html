    Ad Budget vs. Campaign Management Fee

    Digital Marketing Published: 29 September 2026 1 min read MAX SYSTEM Team

    Mixing the two is a common source of misunderstanding when hiring an agency. Here is what to know.

    The advertising budget

    This is the money you pay the platform (Meta, Google or others) for your ads to run. It is usually paid directly from your ad account and you control its limit.

    The management fee

    This pays for the team’s work: setup, tracking, testing, optimization and reporting. It is set in the agreement and is independent of the budget.

    Questions to ask before signing

    Who owns the ad account? How is the budget paid? Which reports are provided and when? Clarity on these points prevents surprises later.
